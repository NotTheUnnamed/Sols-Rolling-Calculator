How to report an issue

Title

Say what broke and when. For example, “Roll button freezes after a biome change.”

Description

List the steps to reproduce it, what you expected, and what happened. Add your device, browser, and simulator version, plus any screenshots or error messages.

Labels

  • Bug for something that isn’t working.
  • Fatal Bug for game-breaking issues.
  • Help when more details are needed.
  • Won't Fix is a maintainer decision.

If labels are unavailable, include the issue type in your description.
